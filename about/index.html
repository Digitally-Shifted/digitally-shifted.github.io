About

The square is the business. The shift is the move.

Digitally Shifted moves a business from no website, or an old website, to a working, managed web presence. The name describes the change. The mark shows it.

The mark

A square, cut by one 45-degree shift.

The square is the business. The diagonal cut through it is the shift into its next state. The arm and the notch share one measure and the gap runs on the 45-degree line, so nothing in the mark is drawn by eye.

How we work

Plain. Precise. Shifted.

  1. 01

    Plain

    Short words and short sentences. If you have to read something twice, we rewrite it.

  2. 02

    Precise

    We say what you get and what it costs. We never invent numbers, clients or results.

  3. 03

    Shifted

    We talk about the change your business gets, not about our process.

Why the site moves like this

Every movement follows the cut.

Things on this site enter on the same 45-degree line as the mark: up and to the right, eight pixels at a time. One entrance per section, never a fade on every card.
